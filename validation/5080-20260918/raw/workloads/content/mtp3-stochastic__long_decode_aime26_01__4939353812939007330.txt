Let \(T\) be the total time, in hours, from Patrick’s start until all three arrive at the park.

  D = pT.

  \[
  D = (p+2)(T-1).

  \[
  D = (p+9)(T-2).

\[
D = pT = \frac{18}{5}\cdot \frac{14}{5} = \frac{252}{25}.

\[
