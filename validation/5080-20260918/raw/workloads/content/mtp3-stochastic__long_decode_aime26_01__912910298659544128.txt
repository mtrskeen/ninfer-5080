Let Patrick’s walking rate be \(p\) miles per hour, and let his total travel time be \(t\) hours.

Then:

- Patrick: distance \(= pt\)
- Tanya: started 1 hour later, so her time is \(t-1\), and her rate is \(p+2\)
- Jose: started 2 hours after Patrick, so his time is \(t-2\), and his rate is \(p+2+7=p+9\)

Since all three cover the same distance,

\[
pt=(p+2)(t-1)
\]

Expanding:

\[
pt=pt-p+2t-2
\]

\[
p=2t-2
\]

Also,

\[
pt=(p+9)(t-2)
\]

Expanding:

\[
pt=pt-2p+9t-18
\]

\[
2p=9t-18
\]

Substitute \(p=2t-2\):

\[
2(2t-2)=9t-18
\]

\[
4t-4=9t-18
\]

\[
5t=14
\]

\[
t=\frac{14}{5}
\]

Then

\[
p=2\cdot \frac{14}{5}-2=\frac{28}{5}-\frac{10}{5}=\frac{18}{5}
\]

So the distance is

\[
D=pt=\frac{18}{5}\cdot \frac{14}{5}=\frac{252}{25}
\]

Thus \(m=252\) and \(n=25\), so

\[
m+n=252+25=277
\]

\[
\boxed{277}
\]
